## The entry

> The Roomba has me pinned beneath the sofa. It keeps reporting me as furniture.

A lost sock's status update from its hiding place under the sofa, where the Roomba has filed it under "furniture". `main.py` writes this one line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
